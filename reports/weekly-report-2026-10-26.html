What changed & recommended next step

Spending was flat at about $143, but more of it turned into real leads: 6 phone calls and 1 booked appointment, versus 1 call and no bookings the week before. That puts the cost at roughly $143 per booked appointment, or about $20 per lead if calls and bookings are counted together. Clicks rose to 114 and each click got cheaper ($1.26). The "28 conversions" headline is still mostly page-load Contacts and should not be treated as leads. The odd overseas Direct traffic from last week went away.

Recommended next steps (no changes made): (1) Look at the "Needs attention" status on the Contact and Book appointment conversion actions and make sure bookings are tracked reliably; (2) the Search campaign is hitting its $15/day budget about a quarter of the time and Google says it is missing relevant keywords, so review keyword coverage before raising budget; (3) check why the Performance Max asset groups are policy-limited and decide whether that $6/day test is worth continuing; (4) sign back into Bing Webmaster Tools so AI-citation tracking can resume.
