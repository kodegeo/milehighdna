What changed & recommended next step

Spending climbed to $156 for the week, mostly because the Performance Max test spent more than it did last week and the main Search campaign ran at full budget. Google Ads counted 15 conversions, but 13 are page-load "Contacts" and do not mean a customer reached out. The meaningful results were one booked appointment and one phone call, which puts the cost per booked appointment at about $156. Real visitor activity was steady: Denver-area traffic is healthy and the bot-like Singapore traffic dropped, but phone taps fell to 7 from 13.

Recommended next step (no changes made by this report): (1) Decide whether to keep the Performance Max test, since it used 33% of spend with no sign of real leads; (2) change the "Contact" conversion so it no longer counts as a primary goal, so reported conversions reflect real bookings and calls; (3) re-sign in to Bing Webmaster Tools in Chrome so next week's report can include AI citations.
