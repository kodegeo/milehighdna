What changed & recommended next step

Google Ads spend and clicks were almost identical to last week (about $158 and 111 clicks). Real leads held steady at 5 (2 booked appointments and 3 phone calls versus 3 and 2 last week), so the cost of a real lead stayed around $32. Because one fewer appointment was booked, cost per booked appointment rose to about $79. With counts this small, a one-booking swing moves that number a lot, so this is normal week-to-week noise rather than a problem.

Website traffic dropped about 20% (193 sessions vs. 245), mostly from fewer short "Direct" visits, which were likely low quality anyway. Phone-click taps recorded on the site fell from 10 to 4. Google Ads still counted 3 calls, so this is worth watching rather than acting on. The GA4 tag problem from last week is still there, so website-side totals should keep being read with caution. The Search campaign is now missing over a quarter of its top-of-page opportunities because of its $15/day budget.

Recommended next step: Make no budget or campaign changes this week. The priority is still fixing the GA4 "Configuration" tag in Google Tag Manager so website numbers can be trusted, and having someone sign in to Bing Webmaster Tools in Chrome so AI-citation tracking can resume. Also do a quick check that the Search campaign's bid strategy is still "Maximize clicks" with the $3 cap, since Google's recommendation describes it as a Maximize conversions campaign. If real leads stay around 5 a week for another 2–3 weeks, a modest Search budget increase would be reasonable to discuss.
