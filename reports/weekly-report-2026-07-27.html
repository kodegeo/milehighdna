5. What changed & recommended next step

This was the first full week with paid search switched off — the pause made on July 19 from the mobile app held all week. The direct effects are visible everywhere: $0 spend, zero ad-driven leads, and about a third fewer visitors overall. The encouraging part is what remained: organic search grew (+33% new users from Google organic), phone clicks held at 3 — the same as last week even without ads — and Bing's AI citation activity picked up noticeably in the last week, including two citations of the prenatal paternity page, the highest-margin service. In short, the free channels held the floor, but nothing is currently feeding the top of the funnel beyond organic.

Recommended next step: (1) Decide whether the pause stands. The 30-day ads review (~Aug 12) will otherwise arrive with two weeks of silence instead of evidence — if the intent was to stop wasteful spend, consider re-enabling only "Search – DNA Testing Denver" at $15/day, which was producing conversions at roughly $12 each before the pause. (2) The Calendly booking conversion has never fired — before spending again, verify the book_appointment/calendly_open tags actually work (test a booking end-to-end), otherwise the metric that matters most stays invisible. (3) Small cleanup: fix which GA4 events are marked "key" so weekly lead counts are trustworthy. No campaign, tag, or site changes were made by this report.
