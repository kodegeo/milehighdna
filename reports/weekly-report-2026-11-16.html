What changed & recommended next step

Spending was about the same as the prior week ($158 vs. $151), but Google Ads delivered more real leads: 3 booked appointments and 2 phone calls, compared with 1 and 1 in the last saved report. That brings cost per booked appointment down to about $53, the best figure in recent weeks. With only 3 bookings, this can swing a lot week to week, so it's an encouraging sign rather than a trend yet. The Search campaign is now losing about a quarter of its top-of-page visibility to its $15/day budget, so demand is there if we choose to spend more later. Performance Max is still policy-limited.

The bigger concern is measurement. GA4 is recording a strange "GA4 – Configuration" event on almost every visit, near-100% engagement, a jump in 3-second "Direct" visits, and one $0 purchase. Something in the tag setup appears to have changed recently, and until it's fixed the website numbers are unreliable. Google Ads' own booking and call counts look sensible and are the numbers to trust this week.

Recommended next step: Make no budget or campaign changes. Have someone check Google Tag Manager's recent version history for a change to the GA4 configuration tag, and confirm whether the $0 purchase on the store was real. Separately, sign in to Bing Webmaster Tools in Chrome so AI-citation tracking can resume (it has been unavailable for over a month). Keep an eye on the Search campaign's budget-limited share. If bookings hold near this level for 2–3 more weeks, a small budget increase would be worth discussing.
